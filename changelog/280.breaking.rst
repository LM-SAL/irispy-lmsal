Removed the cube methods that repeated a function: call ``irispy.utils.dust.remove_dust`` for ``SJICube.remove_dust``, ``irispy.utils.cosmic_rays.remove_cosmic_rays`` for ``SJICube.remove_cosmic_rays`` and ``SpectrogramCube.remove_cosmic_rays``, and ``irispy.utils.rgb.plot_rgb`` for ``SpectrogramCube.plotter.plot_rgb``; ``irispy.visualization.SpectrogramPlotter`` is gone and ``SpectrogramCube`` plots with ``irispy.visualization.IRISPlotter``.
Removed ``SJICube.apply_dust_mask`` and ``SJICube.dust_masked``: ``remove_dust(cube, temporal_window=0, fallback=None)`` returns the cube with its dust masked and its values kept.
