`~irispy.io.read_files` and `~irispy.io.read_spectrograph_lvl2` now read the raster files that the archive writes with no data, such as those of a data gap, instead of raising on their empty ``DATE_OBS``, so the observation loads with those scans in place and every sample masked. Their exposures are timed at ``STARTOBS`` plus the auxiliary ``TIME``, their ``meta["DATE_OBS"]`` and ``meta["DATE_END"]`` are the times of their first and last exposures, and their pointing and roll (``XCEN``, ``YCEN`` and ``SAT_ROT``, which these files give as 0) are NaN, with a warning. Raster files whose NUV exposures are all missing also read now. In every raster file, an exposure missing on both detectors has a NaN pointing in the WCS and in ``meta["exposure FOV center"]`` instead of 0.
