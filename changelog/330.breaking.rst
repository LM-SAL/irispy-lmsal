``irispy.utils.rgb.calculate_rgb`` and ``irispy.utils.rgb.plot_rgb`` now take ``velocity_range``, the Doppler velocities from ``rest_wavelength`` mapped to the blue and red ends of the visible range, such as ``(-200, 200) * u.km / u.s``, in place of ``wavelength_min`` and ``wavelength_max``. A range that holds no sample of the window now raises a ``ValueError`` instead of giving a black image, also the default ``(-100, 100) * u.km / u.s`` on a window that does not cover the rest wavelength, which used the whole window; pass ``velocity_range=None`` for the whole window.
