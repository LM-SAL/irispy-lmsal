`~irispy.sji.SJICube.to_maps` now builds each map from its frame's FITS header, so slit-jaw maps are `~sunpy.map.sources.SJIMap` (AIA and Hinode/SOT maps are their sunpy classes when sunpy knows the instrument) and carry the data unit and the frame's own exposure time; they were plain `~sunpy.map.GenericMap` objects with no unit and the file-level ``EXPTIME`` in every frame. Frames requested out of time order, such as ``to_maps([-1, 0])``, now come back in that order with their own times; the sequence used to sort them by time but date them in the requested order. Maps refuse the unscaled data of a ``memmap=True`` read, whose raw integers they labeled DN.
