``SJICube.to_maps`` now builds each map from its frame's FITS header, so slit-jaw maps are ``SJIMap`` (AIA and Hinode/SOT maps are their sunpy classes when sunpy knows the instrument) and carry the cube's unit, with DN for the IRIS data numbers, and the frame's own exposure time; they were plain ``GenericMap`` objects with no unit and the file-level ``EXPTIME`` in every frame. Frames requested out of time order, such as ``to_maps([-1, 0])``, now come back in that order with their own times; the sequence used to sort them by time but date them in the requested order. Maps refuse the unscaled data of a ``memmap=True`` read.
