The summary printed for an ``SJICube`` now shows the passband as ``1330`` instead of ``1330.0`` (the filter of AIA cubes, such as ``193_THIN`` instead of ``193``, and of Hinode/SOT cubes, such as ``G band 4305`` instead of ``0``), the time of the last frame of a cube sliced along a spatial axis, such as ``cube[:, 5]``, instead of the first frame's twice, and only the axis types of its WCS, without those of the extra coordinates.
