``irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments``, ``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry``, ``irispy.utils.rgb.calculate_rgb`` and ``irispy.utils.rgb.plot_rgb`` now default the rest wavelength of a cube that does not cover ``cube.meta.rest_wavelength`` to the one documented IRIS line it covers, lines within 0.1 Å of each other counting as one, at the strongest, so a crop of the Mg II k window to Mg II h no longer gets velocities 786 km/s too high, nor a crop of the Si IV 1403 window to O IV and S IV 1404.81 velocities 435 km/s too high, nor a crop of the Mg II k window to the Mg II lines at 2798.75 and 2798.82 Å velocities 281 km/s too high, nor a crop of the C II window to C II 1334 velocities 264 km/s too low.
``calculate_moments`` records the rest wavelength of its velocity maps in their ``meta["moments_rest_wavelength"]``.
