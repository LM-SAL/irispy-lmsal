``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`` now divides the asymmetry by the interpolated profile at the peak, not by its maximum, and weights each velocity of the interpolated profile by how much of the ``dv`` about it lies in ``velocity_range``. For the Si IV 1403 window of the gallery example's raster, with ``velocity_range=(25, 75) * u.km / u.s`` and ``min_intensity=75 * u.DN``, the median absolute asymmetry with the default ``dv`` was 34% larger than with ``dv=1 * u.km / u.s``; it is now within 1%. The red and blue wings of a range such as ``(52, 58) * u.km / u.s`` now span the same velocities.
