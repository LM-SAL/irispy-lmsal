`~irispy.utils.fitting.maps_from_fit` now flags a parameter at its bound (`~irispy.utils.fitting.FitQualityFlag.AT_BOUND`) with a tolerance for each spectrum, so a few runaway fits no longer flag the ordinary fits of the rest of the map. It also flags parameters that the fitter reports as at a bound (``active_mask``, as `~astropy.modeling.fitting.TRFLSQFitter` gives) and parameters beyond a bound, as `~astropy.modeling.fitting.LMLSQFitter` returns them.
