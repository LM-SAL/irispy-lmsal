Wavelength axes of spectrograph plots now label ticks to 0.01 nm, so a narrow window, such as Fe XII 1349, gets ticks every 0.02 to 0.05 nm instead of every 0.1 nm.
Tick labels that would overlap are left out, so a narrow window plotted with the default equal aspect labels one or two of its ticks; with ``aspect="auto"`` every tick is labeled.
