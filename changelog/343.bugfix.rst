A 2D map of a scanning raster, such as ``cube[:, :, 10]``, a crop at one wavelength or a moment map is now plotted with its true proportions unless ``aspect`` is given, where its pixels were drawn square: with ``plot_axes=["x", "y"]``, a raster of 2 arcsec steps was 12 times too narrow. Maps of a sit-and-stare, whose steps are times, keep square pixels.
