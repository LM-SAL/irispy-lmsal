``irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`` takes ``velocity_range``, ``(lower, upper)`` Doppler velocities such as ``(-100, 100) * u.km / u.s``, in place of ``wings``, and needs it: ``calculate_moments(cube)`` raises a ``TypeError``, and ``velocity_range=None`` uses the whole window, as ``calculate_moments(cube)`` did. ``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`` loses ``continuum_windows``, subtract a background first with ``irispy.utils.spectrograph.subtract_background``, and its ``degree`` is now ``spline_degree``, recorded in the result ``meta`` as ``rba_spline_degree``.
