``min_intensity`` of ``irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`` and ``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry``, and ``vmin`` and ``vmax`` of ``irispy.utils.rgb.calculate_rgb`` and ``irispy.utils.rgb.plot_rgb``, now take an ``astropy.units.Quantity``; a plain number, which was read in the unit of the cube, raises a ``TypeError``. ``u.DN`` stands for the data's DN, so ``75 * u.DN`` works on level 2 data and ``75 * u.DN / u.s`` on data corrected for exposure time, where both raised a ``UnitConversionError``. ``velocity_range`` of ``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`` must be one ``Quantity``, such as ``(25, 75) * u.km / u.s``; a tuple of two Quantities raises a ``TypeError``.
