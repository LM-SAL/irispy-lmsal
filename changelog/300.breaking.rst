``wobble_cadence`` of ``irispy.utils.wobble.generate_wobble_movie`` is now a time ``astropy.units.Quantity`` that defaults to ``180 * u.s``, instead of a number of seconds, and ``wobble_cadence`` over the median time between frames is rounded to the nearest integer, halves up, instead of rounded down, so a cadence of 2.7 frames shows every third frame instead of every second.
