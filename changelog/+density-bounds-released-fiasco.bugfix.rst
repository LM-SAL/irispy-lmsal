`~irispy.utils.density.density_diagnostic` now works with released fiasco (``fiasco>=0.5.0``, the new minimum for the ``density`` extra), and raises a `ValueError` for a wavelength within 0.02 Å of more than one transition, such as O IV 1401.149 and 1401.163 Å, instead of picking one of them. A density bound whose ratio plus or minus its uncertainty lies off the theoretical curve is now the end of the curve, as in SolarSoft's ``iris_ne_oiv``, instead of a copy of the other bound.
