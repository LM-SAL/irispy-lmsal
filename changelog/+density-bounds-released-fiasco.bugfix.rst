`~irispy.utils.density.density_diagnostic` now works with released fiasco. Instead of picking the nearest transition, it raises a `ValueError` for a wavelength within 0.02 Å of more than one transition, unless the wavelength is the ion's CHIANTI wavelength of one of them, for example 1401.163 Å for O IV. Pass 1401.163 Å where you used 1401.157 Å, as the O IV gallery example in irispy 0.9.1 did, which now raises. A density bound whose ratio plus or minus its uncertainty lies off the theoretical curve is now the end of the curve, as in SolarSoft's ``iris_ne_oiv``, instead of a copy of the other bound.
