`~irispy.utils.density.density_diagnostic` now works with released fiasco. Instead of picking the nearest transition, it adds together the lines of the ion within 0.02 Å of each requested wavelength, such as O IV 1401.149 and 1401.163 Å for 1401.157 Å, and raises a `ValueError` when there are none. A density bound whose ratio plus or minus its uncertainty lies off the theoretical curve is now the end of the curve, as in SolarSoft's ``iris_ne_oiv``, instead of a copy of the other bound.
