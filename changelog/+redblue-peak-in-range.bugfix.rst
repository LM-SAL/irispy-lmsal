``irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`` now looks for the peak of each profile, and tests ``min_intensity`` against it, only within ``velocity_range[1]`` of ``rest_wavelength``, and raises a ``ValueError`` when no sample lies there, as for a ``rest_wavelength`` the cube does not cover. It took the brightest sample of the whole window, which can be another line, the end of the window or a spike: for 23% of the pixels it measured on the Si IV map of the gallery example, while it flagged 22% of the pixels of the Mg II k window of the 2018-01-02 gallery cutout ``INCOMPLETE_WINGS`` for a peak near the end of the window. ``irispy.utils.red_blue.RBAQualityFlag.PEAK_AT_EDGE`` now flags a peak at the first or last of the samples searched where the profile still rises beyond it, and ``irispy.utils.red_blue.RBAQualityFlag.NO_FINITE_DATA`` a pixel with no finite sample among them.
