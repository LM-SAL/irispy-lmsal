``irispy.utils.cosmic_rays.remove_cosmic_rays`` now keeps the value of masked and non-finite samples, and with ``method="astroscrappy"`` that of the samples ``inmask`` marks, instead of setting them to 0 (``"astroscrappy"``) or to NaN or the median of their unmasked neighbors (``"rsliding"``). Saturated samples, which the readers now mask, keep their value too; both methods could replace them.
