``irispy.utils.calculate_dust_mask`` is now ``irispy.utils.dust.calculate_dust_mask``; it takes a slit-jaw cube and returns the IRIS pipeline's bad pixels for its channel, placed in the cube's frame, instead of thresholding the data values, which flagged most of a FUV frame but only the dark cores of the dust, about half its pixels, in 2832 images.
