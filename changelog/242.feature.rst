`irispy.utils.fitting.starts_from_model` now starts a `~astropy.modeling.polynomial.Polynomial1D` background at each spectrum's level, as it does for constant and linear backgrounds, and takes that level with the slope or higher terms of the background removed, so that a steep slope does not lower it. It finds each line's peak above the started background at each wavelength rather than above its level alone, keeps the start of a line with no sample beyond the background on its side, and raises a `ValueError` for a model that is not a sum of its components.
