`irispy.utils.fitting.starts_from_model` now starts a `~astropy.modeling.polynomial.Polynomial1D` background at each spectrum's level, as it does for constant and linear backgrounds. It finds each line's peak above the started background at each wavelength rather than above its level alone, keeps the start of a line with no sample beyond the background on its side, and raises a `ValueError` for a model that is not a sum of its components.
