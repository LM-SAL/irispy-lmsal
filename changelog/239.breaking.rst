The level 2 and mosaic readers no longer set the samples at the level 2 ceiling, ``irispy.utils.constants.SATURATION_LIMIT``, to ``+inf``: they keep their value, are masked and are marked in ``meta["saturated"]``. `~irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`, `~irispy.utils.mg_features.calculate_mg_features`, `~irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`, `~irispy.utils.moments.average_window` and `~irispy.utils.spectrograph.subtract_background` take a sample as saturated when it is both masked and marked, so ``+inf`` no longer counts as saturated, and unmasking a saturated sample uses its value. The burst finders use saturated samples at their value, as IDL does, instead of always counting them as bright.
