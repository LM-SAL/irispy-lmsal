The level 2 and mosaic readers keep the samples at the level 2 ceiling, ``irispy.utils.constants.SATURATION_LIMIT``, at their value instead of setting them to ``+inf``, so that viewers that skip non-finite values show them, and mark them in the mask and in ``meta["saturated"]``. `~irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`, `~irispy.utils.mg_features.calculate_mg_features`, `~irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry`, `~irispy.utils.moments.average_window` and `~irispy.utils.spectrograph.subtract_background` take the saturated samples from the two, so ``+inf`` in the data no longer flags a pixel saturated.
