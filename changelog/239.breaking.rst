The level 2 and mosaic readers now mask the samples clipped at the level 2 ceiling, ``irispy.utils.constants.SATURATION_LIMIT``, keeping their value, and mark them in ``meta["saturated"]``: the saturation flag that ``iris_prep`` sets and the level 2 writer loses. `~irispy.utils.moments.calculate_moments`, `~irispy.utils.mg_features.calculate_mg_features` and `~irispy.utils.red_blue.calculate_red_blue_asymmetry` take a sample as saturated when it is both masked and marked, and flag the pixels with such a sample in a ``"saturated"`` map or the ``SATURATED`` quality flag; `~irispy.utils.moments.average_window` masks such pixels, `~irispy.utils.spectrograph.subtract_background` keeps the saturated samples of a spectrum it has too few samples to fit, and the mark survives background subtraction, exposure time correction and calibration. The burst finders use saturated samples at their value, as IDL does, and unmasking a saturated sample uses its value.
