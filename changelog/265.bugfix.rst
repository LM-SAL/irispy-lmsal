The default rest wavelength of spectrograph windows, `irispy.meta.SGMeta.rest_wavelength`, now comes from the IRIS line catalog; continuum windows such as ``2832`` keep ``TWAVE``.
Default Doppler velocities are now 16.3 km/s lower for Mg II k, 6.7 km/s higher for Fe XII 1349 and 4.3 km/s higher for Si IV 1394.
