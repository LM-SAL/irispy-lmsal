OBS 3824262996: Very large dense raster 131.7x175 400s, no spatial summing, near the limb, so
the upper fiducial mark (row 777) is off the disk.

Kept:   the Si IV 1403 window (extension 5), step 225 (of 400), all 1095 slit rows and
        wavelength pixels 150-169 (of 304), 1402.45-1402.93 A. Indices are 0-based. The data
        values (raw int16 with BSCALE/BZERO) are unchanged, and the auxiliary and level 1
        filename tables keep the row of that step.
        The step and pixels are chosen so that the mark, at row 238.06, stands out from the noise
        less than every other dip: the candidates find_fiducials considers are rows 32.00, 53.76,
        176.45 (the FUV dark row that is not a mark) and 238.06, with significance 28.9, 10.4,
        8.1 and 6.9. Rows 120-299 hold only the dark row and the mark.
Header: NWIN = 1 and the window keywords of Si IV 1403 are numbered 1. NAXIS1/3, CRPIX1/3 and
        TWMIN1/TWMAX1 describe the cut; NEXP = NRASTERP = 1. CDELT3, STEPS_AV/STEPT_AV, DATE_OBS
        and DATE_END still describe the whole file. The filename table declares TFIELDS = 9 for
        its 9 columns (the whole file says 7, see tools/compress.py). TSR1/TER1 (1 and 1095)
        still describe the whole window, which the cut keeps along the slit, as do TSC1/TEC1,
        STEPS_DV/STEPT_DV and the statistics keywords (DATA*, TD*1).
